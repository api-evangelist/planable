Using ChatGPT for social media can take you from a blank caption to a working draft in the time it would normally take to open your notes app. The real bottleneck shows up next: prompts without enough context return output that could belong to any brand or campaign. Better prompts, built with the right platform and audience detail, change the output quality significantly.
