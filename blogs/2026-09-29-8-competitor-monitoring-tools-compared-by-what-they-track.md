Competitor monitoring tools track a rival’s online activity on a schedule: social performance, search rankings, website traffic, prices, page changes, and how AI answers describe them. But each tool gets you only one piece of the puzzle, in most cases: one only covers social analytics, while another strictly focuses on AI brand visibility, for example. Surpassing competitors involves knowing them through and through, and a single platform just won’t cut it.
