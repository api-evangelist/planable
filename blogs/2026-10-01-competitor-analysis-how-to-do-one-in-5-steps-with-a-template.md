Competitor analysis is a practice almost everybody does, but not everyone really puts it down on paper properly. SEOs, product teams, and social media marketers probably notice continuous shifts in a competitor’s product positioning, messaging, and general marketing strategy. But these details often remain decentralized, scattered across countless tabs and spreadsheets that teams across departments may have no clue about.
