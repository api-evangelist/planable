For nearly two years, I posted on LinkedIn whenever I had something ready, then went quiet again. I never posted enough to know which topics or formats actually worked for my audience. Meanwhile, I was creating content almost every day for SaaS brands, from SEO and AI search articles to case studies and social posts.
