The chart dips, and many social media sentiment analysis tools stop right there: the reply happens in another inbox, and the client or location report gets rebuilt by hand somewhere else. Scoring the mood is the easy part of social media sentiment analysis. The friction hides in two places: sarcasm that gets scored as praise, and a sentiment feed that sits one tool away from where your team approves and publishes.
