If your content approval process currently lives in a haunted house (a Google Doc, three email threads, two Slack channels, and one comment that just says “not sure about this”), you don’t need “more communication.” You need a content collaboration tool. A good content collaboration platform gives marketing teams one place to draft, share, and ship work with file sharing, group or private chats, in-context comments, internal notes, version control, and stakeholder approvals that don’t require a detective.
