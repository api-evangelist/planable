Sprout Social alternatives are worth a look the moment the invoice starts growing faster than the team. In my testing, the friction shows up in the same places: per-seat pricing that punishes adding a reviewer, social listening sold as a separate add-on, and approval flows built for one in-house team rather than five clients. I’ve spent seven years inside B2B marketing tools, and I know where the friction hides: in who can approve what, how feedback reaches the post, and what happens to the bill when a client brings three more stakeholders.
