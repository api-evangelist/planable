Social listening tools track conversations across social media and other online channels to show what people are saying about your brand, competitors, or any other industry-related topic. But not every platform covers the same use cases. Some focus mainly on branded mentions and customer feedback, while others support broader competitor analysis, trend monitoring, and market research.
