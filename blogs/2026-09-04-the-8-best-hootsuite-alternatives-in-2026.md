The search for robust Hootsuite alternatives is growing as the platform shows its age. In my testing, the same friction kept coming up: a dashboard that feels cluttered, social profiles that need reconnecting at the worst moment, and core features (analytics, approval processes, reporting) that get expensive fast. I’ve spent six years inside social media management platforms and I know where the friction hides: permissions, reconnect loops, and features that break the moment you scale.
